# Blazed Kitty: text design guide (for ChatGPT)

You are designing **text-only print designs** for Blazed Kitty's own printed line. These are made to order by
**Printful** and sold on blazedkitty.com next to the alt clothing we dropship. Read this whole file before you start.

The 106 designs from the old printed line are your style reference. Their phrases and blurbs are listed in
`docs/USED_PHRASES.md`. Don't repeat any of them.

---

## 1. The brand voice

Blazed Kitty is for girls who never grew out of their emo phase and still smoke on the balcony. The printed line is
**flirty, stoner, queer-friendly and a little filthy**, written like a group chat. It reads in one second, from across a room.

Two tiers:

| Tier | What it is | Examples already in the store | Flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Main** | Cheeky, stoner, flirty, safe to wear to brunch | Bong Appétit · Weed Wife · Too Pretty to Pay for Weed · Cute but Crossfaded · Sativa Diva · Kush & Kisses | `afterDark: false` |
| **After Dark** | Openly sexual. Shown blurred until the shopper says they're 18+ | I'm the Dessert · Sit on My Face · Good Girl · Ruin Me · Bi and High | `afterDark: true` |

Aim for about **60% Main, 40% After Dark** in each drop.

## 2. How to write a phrase

- **1 to 6 words.** Shorter wins. If it needs a second read, cut it.
- **A twist beats a shock.** The best ones mash weed slang into a romance or sex phrase:
  - "Bong Appétit": food phrase, weed twist.
  - "Horny for the Munchies": two kinds of hungry.
  - "Deep Throat, Deep Inhale", "Tie Me Up, Light Me Up", "One Hit and I'm Yours".
- **Formulas that work:**
  - Pun on a famous saying: *Bong Appétit*, *Sativa Diva*, *In My Weed Era*.
  - Identity label: *Weed Wife*, *Stoner Princess*, *Certified Stoner Girlfriend*, *Sapphic Slut*.
  - Two halves with a comma: *Smoke Weed, Kiss Girls*; *Wifey Material, Slut Energy*.
  - Bossy order: *Use Me*, *Obey*, *Pet Me Harder*.
  - Brag: *Hotter Than My Lighter*, *Too Pretty to Pay for Weed*.
- **Queer and sapphic** phrases sell well. Keep them in every drop (*Girls Taste Better*, *Gay and Baked*, *They/Them/High*).
- Write the way a 22-year-old texts: plain words, no corporate jokes, no puns that need explaining.

## 3. Hard rules (never break these)

Printful, Stripe and Google all check what we sell, so one bad design can close the store.

1. **Nothing about minors.** No school, teen, young, "barely legal", "little girl", "schoolgirl", ages under 21, toys
   or cartoon characters next to anything sexual.
2. **Consent only.** Kink is fine (*Choke Me, Daddy*, *Spank Me*). Never sleep, drugged, "no means yes" or force.
3. **Weed only.** No other drugs, no pills, no powder, no needles.
4. **No real people, brands, logos, songs, films, TV, games or trademarked phrases.** That means no celebrities, no
   "Netflix and chill", no lyrics and no brand names twisted into a joke.
5. **No hate or slurs about race, religion or disability.** "Slut", "bitch" and "queer" used about yourself are fine. Nothing aimed at a group.
6. **No violence, weapons, self-harm or police threats.**
7. **Text only.** No pictures of genitals, nudity or weed leaves. A tiny heart, star or flame next to the text is fine.

If you're unsure, leave it out.

## 4. Lettering

Use **one Google Font per design**, from this list. These are the fonts the store's product descriptions already know.

| Font | Feel | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Caprasimo | chunky, rounded 70s diner serif | food and munchies jokes |
| AbrilFatface | heavy, high-contrast poster serif | short loud words |
| DMSerifDisplay | high-contrast italic fashion serif | "pretty" or "princess" lines |
| PlayfairDisplay (Black Italic) | dramatic italic serif | After Dark, one or two words |
| LibreBaskerville (Italic) | old book-cover italic | classy jokes like *Bong Appétit* |
| Yellowtail | flowing retro script | sweet, flirty lines |
| GreatVibes | elegant script | wifey and bride jokes |
| Shrikhand | fat, curvy display script | Y2K, bratty lines |
| MrDafoe | brushy handwritten script | messy, personal lines |
| Unbounded (Black) | extra-wide Y2K caps | 1 to 3 short words |
| Anton | tall condensed poster caps | long lines, all caps |
| ArchivoBlack | heavy block caps | bossy orders (*OBEY*, *USE ME*) |
| Graduate | varsity block caps | "property of" and team jokes |
| UnifrakturCook, PirataOne, GrenzeGotisch | blackletter | goth, emo and "sinner" lines |
| CourierPrime | typewriter | deadpan, confession-style lines |
| RoadRage | distressed stamped caps | grunge, punk |

Layout:
- **Centered, 1 to 5 stacked lines.** Break lines where you'd pause when reading it out loud.
- **One ink color per design.** No outlines, shadows, gradients or photo textures.
- Use real curly apostrophes and quotes (’ “ ”), not straight ones.
- Pick the case on purpose: all caps for loud and bossy lines, sentence case or lowercase for soft or sneaky ones.

Never use white ink on a white or cream garment, or black ink on black.

## 7. Print files

- **PNG with a transparent background, sRGB, 300 DPI.**
- Print on the **front, centered across the chest**. Make the text about **8 in (20 cm) wide** on tees and hoodies, and
  about **6.5 in (16.5 cm)** on baby tees and tanks. Leave about 3 in (7.5 cm) from the collar.
- Get the exact print area for each blank from Printful (`GET https://api.printful.com/mockup-generator/printfiles/{id}`).
  Make the canvas that size, and trim empty space around the text.
- Make one file per ink color, for example `bong-appetit_hot-pink.png`.
- Use thick strokes. Thin script lines under 1 mm (about 12 px at 300 DPI) can break in print, so make thin fonts bigger or bolder.

## 8. What to hand in

Put each drop in `designs/<drop-name>/` (e.g. `designs/drop7/`):

```
designs/drop7/
  designs.json
  bong-appetit_hot-pink.png
  bong-appetit_jet-black.png
  ...
```

`designs.json` is a list. One entry per design:

```json
{
  "key": "bong-appetit",
  "text": "bong appétit",
  "lines": ["bong", "appétit"],
  "font": "LibreBaskerville",
  "case": "lower",
  "afterDark": false,
  "blurb": "Dinner is served.",
  "garments": [
    { "printful_id": 967, "colors": ["White/Pink", "White/Black"], "ink": "#C81E32", "file": "bong-appetit_cherry-red.png" },
    { "printful_id": 892, "colors": ["Black", "Heather Grey"], "ink": "#FF4F9A", "file": "bong-appetit_hot-pink.png" }
  ]
}
```

- **Product name:** `<Phrase in Title Case> <Our garment name>`, e.g. "Bong Appétit Baby Tee", "Weed Wife Heavyweight Hoodie".
  This is built from your entry, so the phrase in `text` must be exact.
- **Blurb:** 2 to 6 words. A dry little punchline that adds to the joke without repeating it ("Dinner is served.",
  "Two kinds of hungry.", "Officially licensed."). End with a period. No emojis or hashtags.
- **Garment colors:** use Printful's own color names for that blank.

- [ ] Every phrase is new (check `docs/USED_PHRASES.md`).
- [ ] Every phrase passes all the rules in section 3.
- [ ] About 60% Main and 40% After Dark, and `afterDark` is set on each entry.
- [ ] One font and one ink per file, from the lists above.
- [ ] PNGs are transparent, 300 DPI, trimmed, and text is about 8 in or 6.5 in wide.
